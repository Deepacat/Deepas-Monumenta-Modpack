Deepa's Monumenta Pack is an unofficial performance, quality of life, polish and immersion focused mod-pack for the Monumenta CTM-MMO-RPG Minecraft server.

# Welcome to Monumenta! https://playmonumenta.com/

Monumenta is a Minecraft survival-mode combat-focused dungeon-crawler with MMO-RPG elements based on the "Complete the Monument" adventure map genre. Fight monsters, get loot, play with friends, Complete the Monuments!

Monumenta is forever free to play, not for profit, and developed by passionate community members for no gain.

The only monetization is patreon supporters to fund the servers continuation, only giving cosmetics and slight server-wide buffs.

## About this Modpack
This modpack is meant to be my optimal immersive new-player experience for the Monumenta server, giving a polished experience without the extra bells and whistles such as extra interface information elements or certain outgoing helper features.

The modpack includes many Monumenta specific mods like the Unofficial Monumenta mod, Monumenta Addons, Monumenta Item Dictionary, USBPlus, and several of vladomemes off-platform mods and forks for Monumenta.

Many other QOL mods and the best of performance mods are also included for a smooth experience on the server.

All of the mods required to use every feature of the official Monumenta resource pack are present, and properly configured to cause no graphical issues, having everything display and perform as intended.

Custom buttons and information prompts are added to certain interfaces for shortcuts to useful Monumenta tools, commands, and helpful information about the Modpack.

Other resource packs and my own edits and fixes of them are included for a clean, immersive, fantasy oriented interface to ensure immersion and visibility.

This pack would also serve as a nice base to edit, adding and removing resourcepacks, mods, configuring, or whatever else you'd like to do with it!

## Important Notes
- The mods in this modpack are **"Use at your own risk"**. This means that Monumentas rules may change, and current features maybe disallowed. It is up to you to disable those disallowed features or mods. However I will try my best to update when rules change. If a mod or feature in a mod is found to not be allowed please contact me ASAP via `deepacat` on discord or the server linked below.
- When updating this modpack note that all configs that might've been updated will not apply their changes to your instance, this is done so you can edit them freely without overwriting. If you want the new files to replace yours, I'd suggest reinstalling the modpack and moving your relevant files over to the new instance.
- I am not affiliated with the developers of Monumenta and this modpack is entirely unofficial! For a more barebones resourcepack with performance mods and the resourcepack essentials, [find the official modpack here!](https://modrinth.com/modpack/official-monumenta-modpack)
- The minimap is disabled by default but can be toggled by pressing "," (Comma) as the default keybind
- This modpack may not always have the latest official resourcepack, use /help rp on the server to check the latest!

## My recommended ingame settings changes to be used with this modpack
(in game on Monumenta click the custom gear button in the inventory next to offhand. Or use `/player settings`)
- **Gameplay/Combat Options** (Diamond Sword)
  - `Ability hotbar` > `disabled` (Paper)
    - Disables the bossbar with abilities, as a mod adds ability icons above the hotbar already
  - **Damage Indicator Options** (Iron Sword)
    - `Mob Health Display` > `Full` (Nametag)
      - Shows enemy health bars
    - `Damage Splashes` > `Show amount and type` (Damage Splashes)
      - Shows damage particles on hit mobs
- **Technical Options** (Comparator)
  - `Spoof world names` > `enabled` (Cartography Table)
    - Required for map auto switching across worlds
  - `Auto Abandon completed dungeons` > `disabled` (Daylight Sensor)
    - Incase you need to come back to a dungeon for something after finishing
- **Trigger/Interactable Options** (Shield)
  - `Block interactions` > `disabled` (Loom)
    - Prevents accidental ability casts with scythes, and opening unwanted interfaces in combat
  - `Offhand swapping` > `disabled` (Left Shield)
  - `Offhand swapping in inventory` > `disabled` (Rightmost Shield)
    - Both offhand keys can be annoying and cause accidental swaps, safer to swap offhand with mouse clicks
- **Filtered pickup and disabled drops** (Dirt)
  - `Item drop settings` (Drop blocking) > `Equipped Items + Hotbar` (Leather)
  - `Item pickup settings` > `Interesting` (Item Pickup)
    - Can toggle all pickups any time with /pu
